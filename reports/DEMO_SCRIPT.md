1. Open the motivation section. Rotate the transmon schematic and point to the junction and capacitor. Ask how long a logical memory can preserve the value we prepared.
2. Follow the eight-stage framework. Stop at Characterization to explain how measured readout behavior affects decoding. Open gate twirling to compare two native rotations.
3. Show the Emerald chip map. Each line is a calibrated direct CZ interaction. The highlighted patch supports the distance-3 circuit without routing SWAPs.
4. Open the three-round hardware comparison. On the shared calibration, Z memory failed in 48 of 1,000 shots at distance 3 and 363 of 1,000 at distance 5. The larger circuit needed 78 routing SWAPs.
5. Show the separate simulation curves and explain their noise assumptions. Close with the four matched 10,000-shot arms. Twirled Qrisp at distance 3 gave the lowest observed failure probability, 5.08%, with overlapping confidence intervals.
